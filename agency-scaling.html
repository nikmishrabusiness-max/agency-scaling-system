F.A.Q.

  What all is included in the education program?

The entire education program has 1) video modules to give you a self paced growth at your comfortable speed, 2) Weekly calls for live help, 3) access to a Google Drive for all the documents & plug-&-plays, 4) Slack workspace to ask all your questions and share wins to celebrate together. 

  Will I get personal support from the mentors/success managers?

Yes. We have weekly calls with the founders/mentors/success managers. On these calls you can ask all your personal obstacles and get customised recommendations. Additionally, we have Slack channels dedicated to asking questions and get help from the mentors and get different perspective of other agency owners.

  Is the program self paced or with a fixed timeline? 

It is self paced. Totally depends on your availability and how much time you're investing towards it. 

  Is there a guarantee?

Yes. We offer a full money back guarantee. In case you work on all the recommended areas and implement the program still don't get any results, we will give you a full refund without hassle. 

  How long will it take me to hit my goals?

No one plan works for everyone in the same pace. The best we have done is to provide you with everything that will work to get you to your revenue goals, it's upto your aptitude and caliber to speed up your journey.

  Is this education program right for me at my stage?

The program has modules from Setting up the Foundations and Mindset to Hiring & Training and smoothening your Day to Day Operations. So, if you're making anything above $1k/2k monthly and want to reach consistent $10k-$20k monthly revenue, the program is for you.
